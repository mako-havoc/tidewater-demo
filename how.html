About this demo

How this shop works

Tidewater Goods is a sample build. It shows a small shop that takes orders through Discord instead of a card checkout. Every item, price and stock count is made up.

What you can try here

  • Catalogue: 24 items in 6 categories, with search, category filters, sorting and an in-stock filter.
  • Versions with their own stock: each item comes in Standard and Premium, and each version has its own count.
  • Price engine: one formula prices everything (base cost × version × category markup, rounded up to .95). Each product page shows the working.
  • Member prices: the header switch shows what a member would pay (at least 10% off, rounded down to a .95 price).
  • Cart and order request: your cart is kept in this browser only. "Preview order request" shows the Discord ticket the shop would receive.
  • Product photos: made with AI for this demo. They show made-up, unbranded products.
  • We buy: a buy-back form with condition grades, an instant estimate and a per-request limit.

Demo vs. a real build

This demo

  • Static pages and two JSON files, hosted for free
  • Cart saved in your browser only
  • Requests show a preview; nothing is sent
  • Member prices are a switch, not an account
  • Stock is fixed in the data file

A client build adds

  • A Discord bot that opens real private tickets, with claim, close and transcripts
  • Stock that updates when staff close a sale
  • A simple page for staff to edit items, costs and stock
  • Member accounts linked to Discord, if wanted
  • The shop's own name, items, pictures and domain

Card checkout isn't part of this package.

Privacy

No cookies and no analytics. Your cart, the member switch and the ticket counter are saved in your own browser's storage and never leave it. The only outside request is for the web fonts, which load from Google Fonts.
