How ordering works

No card checkout. Orders become a private chat with the shop.

  1. Pick your items

    Choose a version, see live stock, add to your cart.

  2. Send a request

    Leave your name and a note. No payment on the website.

  3. A ticket opens

    A private channel opens in the shop's Discord with your order in it.

  4. Staff confirm

    They check stock, reply in the ticket and arrange pickup.

One price formula

Cost, version and category markup set every price, rounded to a .95 ending. Open any item to see the maths.

Flip the switch in the header to see what members pay. It's a demo, so there are no real accounts.
